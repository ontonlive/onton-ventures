## 1. Vision, Mission & Governance Structure

### 1.1 Vision & Mission
* **Vision:** Make ONTON the go-to event & community platform for Telegram and the Web (Luma-level simplicity first, with optional Web3 & cross-chain tools when needed).
* **Mission:** Help any organizer launch, manage, and monetize an event in 60 seconds—using plain Web2 achievements by default, and crypto tickets, SBTs, and interactive formats only when they add real value.

### 1.2 The Three-Rule Core Strategy (Aligned with Review Council `#171`)
1. **Rule 1 — Product Excellence First:** Build a fast, unified, Luma-grade app that works smoothly without visual or technical friction so organizers and attendees stay.
2. **Rule 2 — Organizer & Attendee Value Drives Revenue:** Revenue is not an isolated goal—it is the natural result of solving real needs for organizers and participants, especially helping organizers come back for their **2nd, 3rd, and 10th event** instead of hosting a one-off promo.
3. **Rule 3 — Fair, Data-Driven DAO Governance:** Grow the **ONTON DAO** into a balanced strategic governance body that oversees ecosystem growth, treasury reserves, and fair reward rules based on published quarterly numbers rather than hypothetical schemes.

### 1.3 Three-Chamber DAO Architecture ([Constitution v2.1](https://onton.live/dao))
* **Corporate vs. DAO Boundary:**
  * **ONTON Core Company** retains 100% of software IP, servers, domains, and bots, plus **75% of net fee revenue** for operations and engineering.
  * **ONTON DAO** governs the **25% Net Revenue Buyback & Yield Pool**, the **Eco-Pool Reserve**, co-host event grants, and the **200M `$ONIT` Community DAO Reserve**.
* **The 3 Chambers:**
  1. **Chamber 3 — The Genesis ONION Senate:** Genesis ONION NFT holders (`618` wallets across Platinum `2.0x`, Gold `1.5x`, Silver `1.25x`, Bronze `1.1x`) & `25,000+ $ONIT` stakers. Highest strategic weight, treasury & buyback oversight.
  2. **Chamber 2 — Organizer & Partner Council:** Verified event hosts (`500+ ONTON Points` or `10,000+ $ONIT`, unlocking the **2.5% Pro Organizer Fee Tier**). Focuses on organizer retention, co-host grants, and event tooling.
  3. **Chamber 1 — Citizen Assembly:** Active event attendees & community members (`50+ ONTON Points` or `1,000+ $ONIT`). Focuses on attendee UX, referrals, and community quests.

## 4. OIP-6 Consensus Rules (Genesis NFT Base Yield & Anti-Dump Guardrails)

Following alignment between the Review Council (`#114`–`#130`) and 88-NFT holder `Utya 🦆` / Merusaka (`#1404285`–`#1404499`):

1. **No Splitting Before Real Revenue Floor (`@OneMillionExabytesPerSecond` `#128`):**
   * The 25% DAO Revenue Pool is only split into Layer 1 (Base NFT Yield) and Layer 2 (`$ONIT` Staking Boost) after quarterly net revenue reaches the minimum baseline and is published on `onton.live/dao`.
2. **Source-Agnostic Minimum `$ONIT` Holding (`Merusaka` + `Review Council`):**
   * To protect Getgems secondary market buyers (where a previous owner may have sold the original FairLock tokens) while preventing `"100 NFTs / 0 $ONIT"` extraction, activating Layer 1 Base Yield requires holding or staking at least **`1,000 $ONIT` per `1.0x` NFT weight unit** (`1,000` per Platinum, `500` per Gold, `250` per Silver, `100` per Bronze — counting liquid wallet + staked + unclaimed FairLock `$ONIT`, regardless of source).
3. **Per-Wallet Concentration Cap (`@OneMillionExabytesPerSecond` `#128`):**
   * Matching the 5% DAO voting power cap, Layer 1 payouts are subject to a per-wallet concentration cap so no single whale wallet can drain the pool passively.
4. **Product Demand Utility (`Merusaka` `#1404285`):**
   * Enable buying event/contest tickets with `$ONIT` at a **10–20% discount** compared to TON/Stars, plus `$ONIT` raffles and co-branded drops.
